# Identities

An identity is one person or brand the Content Engine writes for. You can have several (yourself, your company, a client), each with its own setup, ideas and posts. The one you are working on is shown at the top of the sidebar, under **Identity**.

## What an identity is

You sign in with one user account, and that account can own several identities. Each identity is a separate persona or brand: its own profile, voice, goals, ideas and connected accounts. Working on one identity never changes another.

Create a separate identity when posts should come from a different person or brand: your company page next to your personal profile, say, or a client you write for.

If it is the same you on several platforms, you do not need another identity. Pick all those platforms for one identity and give each platform its own style (professional on LinkedIn, fun on Instagram) in [Preferences](/identity/preferences).

## What belongs to each identity

| Belongs to each identity                                     | Belongs to your user account                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Profile, Voice, Knowledge and Preferences                    | Signing in and security                             |
| Goals, ideas, runs and posts                                 | Your Buffer connection, shared by every identity    |
| The schedule and the Brand Theme                             | AI usage limits, counted across all your identities |
| The social accounts it publishes to                          |                                                     |
| Its newsletters, inbox address and the stories they bring in |                                                     |

The app's pages show the active identity's data. To see or change another identity's goals, posts or connected accounts, switch to it first.

## Create another identity

1. Click the identity box at the top of the sidebar and choose **New identity**. The page that opens is titled **Create Another Identity**.
2. **Where you post**: pick every platform this identity posts on (LinkedIn, X, Threads, Facebook, Instagram), then click **Continue**. You need at least one.
3. **About you**: under **What do you post about, and who is it for?**, describe the identity in your own words: who it is, what it posts, who follows it, and anything it promotes. You can also add a profile link for each platform you picked and a **Website**.
4. Click **Set it up with AI** to have the profile and each platform's style filled in for you. It takes about a minute, and **Cancel** stops it. Or click **I'll fill it in myself** to type everything yourself.
5. **Review**: check **Your platforms** (a style for each, and **Edit** to say who follows the identity there and how it sounds), **Posting as**, **Basic information** and **Online presence**. Fields the AI filled are marked **AI** until you change them. **Rewrite** takes you back to the description.
6. Click **Create identity**.

Nothing is saved until you click **Create identity**. The new identity becomes the active one straight away, and your other identities stay as they are.

After it is created you land on the Voice step. If you used **Set it up with AI**, you land on the Profile step instead, where the AI's suggestions for voice, goals, knowledge and preferences open for you to review.

<Tags type="tip">

Your first identity is created the same way. Before you have one, the sidebar shows **Set up identity**, which opens **Create Your Identity**.

</Tags>

<Details summary="I clicked Create identity twice. Do I now have two?">

No. The same name created again within a minute is treated as the same request, so no second identity is made and you are taken to the first one.

</Details>

## Switch between identities

1. Click the identity box at the top of the sidebar. A menu titled **Your identities** opens.
2. Each identity is listed with its initials, its name, and whether it is a **Person** or a **Brand**. The active one has a check mark.
3. Click the identity you want.

You can also switch from the **Identities** page (see below).

Every page now works on that identity. If you were looking at a single post, run or idea, you are taken to the list it came from (Posts, Run Details or Ideas) instead, because that item belongs to the identity you left.

You can also use the keyboard: with the menu open, the arrow keys move between identities and <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> closes it.

When the sidebar is collapsed, the box shows only the identity's initials. Hover over it to see which identity you are working on, and click it to open the same menu.

### Which identity opens next time

Your choice is remembered in this browser until you switch again. On another browser or device, the app opens your oldest identity until you choose one there.

## See all your identities

The **Identities** page lists every identity you own, oldest first, ten to a page. Open it from **Account Setup › Identities** in the sidebar, or choose **Manage identities** in the identity menu.

Each identity shows whether it is a **Person** or a **Brand**, when it was created, and how many posts, ideas and goals it has. The one you are working on is marked **Active**. From its row you can:

- **Switch to**: make it the active identity. You stay on the Identities page.
- **Edit**: open its setup (Profile, Voice, Knowledge and the other steps). Another identity is switched to first, since the setup pages work on the active identity.
- **Delete** (the bin icon): delete it for good. See below.

**New identity**, at the top of the page, creates another one, as described above.

## Delete an identity

1. On the **Identities** page, click the bin icon on the identity's row.
2. Check what will be deleted. The dialog counts its posts, ideas, goals, knowledge documents, runs and connected accounts.
3. Type the identity's name, exactly as shown, and click **Delete identity**.

Everything in the identity is deleted with it, and it can't be undone: its profile, voice, knowledge, goals, ideas, runs, posts and their images, schedule, brand theme, newsletters and connected accounts. Your other identities don't change.

- **Scheduled posts** are called off and will not go out.
- **Posts already published** stay on LinkedIn, X, Threads, Facebook and Instagram. Delete them there if you need to.
- **Its inbox address** stops delivering, and is never given to anyone else.
- **AI usage** it already spent still counts toward your limits.
- If it was the **active** identity, your oldest remaining identity becomes active. If it was your only one, the sidebar offers **Set up identity** again.

<Details summary="Why can't I delete an identity right now?">

Deleting waits while a run is working for the identity, while its posts are being written, or while one of its posts is being sent to a platform. Wait for it to finish (or stop the run on **Run Details**), then delete again.

</Details>
