# FAQ

Short answers to the questions people ask most about the Content Engine. Each answer links to the page with the full story.

## Approval and publishing

<Details summary="Is anything posted without my approval?">

No. A run's ideas wait for your approval before any post is written, and only a post you have approved can be published. **Publish now** asks you to confirm first (your first post shows a full final check you must tick), and a scheduled post goes out only at the time you picked. Auto-publishing is off until you turn it on for a network, and even then it only publishes approved posts planned on the [Schedule](/docs/publishing/schedule) for that day.

</Details>

<Details summary="Do I need a Buffer account?">

To publish from the app, yes. Posts go out through your own Buffer account, which you connect once on [Integrations](/docs/settings/integrations). Without it, you can still use **Copy post** on an approved post and paste it into the platform yourself.

</Details>

<Details summary="Can I cancel a scheduled post?">

Yes. Click **Cancel** next to the post's publishing time. Once it has started publishing, it can no longer be cancelled.

</Details>

<Details summary="Can I delete a published post from the platform through the app?">

No. Open the post on the platform and delete it there. Deleting a post in the app removes it from the app only.

</Details>

<Details summary="What happens to my scheduled posts if my connection breaks?">

They stay scheduled, and a reconnect button appears next to each one. Reconnect Buffer on [Integrations](/docs/settings/integrations) before they are due. Any that fall due while the connection is broken fail, and you can use **Try again** once you have reconnected.

</Details>

## Identities and platforms

<Details summary="Can I manage several brands or people?">

Yes. Each person or company you write for is an identity, with its own profile, voice, goals, ideas, posts, inbox address and publishing accounts. Switch between them, or create one with **New identity**, from **Your identities** in the sidebar. See [Identities](/docs/getting-started/identities).

</Details>

<Details summary="Which platforms does it write for and publish to?">

LinkedIn, X (single posts and threads), Threads, Facebook and Instagram, with text, image and carousel posts. It also makes short AI videos for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels: pick **Short video** when you create posts (see [Short videos](/docs/publishing/posts/post-types#short-videos)). Publishing to each network goes through your Buffer account, as long as your administrator has turned it on.

</Details>

<Details summary="Does signing in with Google let the app post anywhere?">

No. Signing in only tells the app who you are. Publishing to LinkedIn is set up through Buffer on [Integrations](/docs/settings/integrations), like the other networks.

</Details>

## Ideas, drafts and newsletters

<Details summary="Where do the ideas come from?">

From runs. You pick the goals a run works toward; it gathers your identity's context, researches the web and your knowledge base (including stories from the newsletters your inbox receives), and proposes ideas for those goals. See [Starting a Run](/docs/account-setup/runs).

</Details>

<Details summary="Can I edit the drafts?">

Yes. Open a post and use **Edit text** to change it yourself, or **Regenerate** for a new version written by AI. Earlier versions are kept, and you can restore one. A regenerated or restored text needs your approval again. See [Editing a Post](/docs/publishing/posts/editing).

</Details>

<Details summary="How do I get my newsletter address?">

Your identity's address is shown on the Inbox page under **Your address**, made from your name, with a button to copy it. Click the pencil to choose a different name. Your old address keeps delivering, and each identity can have up to five addresses. See [Inbox](/docs/publishing/newsletters/inbox).

</Details>

<Details summary="Does the app subscribe me to newsletters?">

No. **Discover Newsletters** finds newsletters that fit your identity, but you subscribe yourself: click **Open Signup Page**, sign up with your inbox address, then mark it, for example with **Mark as Subscribed**. See [Discover](/docs/publishing/newsletters/discover).

</Details>

## Account and costs

<Details summary="What does it cost?">

The app shows what its AI work costs: in total on the [Dashboard](/docs/getting-started/dashboard), and for each run on its page. Your administrator sets spending limits per person, over 30 days and over 24 hours, and you can see how much of each is used. Publishing uses your own Buffer account, so your Buffer plan applies. See [AI Usage & Limits](/docs/help/ai-usage).

</Details>

<Details summary="Can I sign in with a password?">

No. You sign in with Google or a passkey; there are no passwords. Add a passkey from the account menu or on [Security](/docs/settings/security).

</Details>

<Details summary="Why do my colours look different on another computer?">

Your app colours are kept in the browser where you set them, not in your account. Set them again on [Theme](/docs/settings/theme) in each browser you use.

</Details>
