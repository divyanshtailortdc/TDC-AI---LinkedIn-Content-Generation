# Brand Theme

The Brand Theme sets the logo, colours, typefaces and image style used on every image generated for an identity's posts, so they look like they belong together. Each identity has its own theme.

## How post images are built

With **Like my posts**, the default, each image is made to look like the images you already post on that platform: the same kind of image (a photo, a quote card, an infographic), layout, colours and type. It follows the example posts under each link on the [Profile](/docs/account-setup/profile#example-posts-for-your-images) page. The words on it come from the post, and every word is checked against what was asked for. Carousel slides are made the same way, each slide matching the cover (see [Carousels](/docs/publishing/posts/post-types#carousels)).

The other styles follow one layout: a text side with a short tagline, a headline with its key words highlighted and a supporting line, set in your typefaces and colours on a light background, next to a picture in your image style. With **Minimal Graphic Design**, the picture is a diagram instead. **Like my posts** uses this layout with a photograph until the identity has example posts.

The **Live Canvas Preview** on the right shows a sample image with placeholder words. It changes as you edit, before you save.

## Logo & Visibility

Click the upload area, or drop a file on it, to add your logo. PNG, SVG, WebP and JPEG files are accepted, up to 5 MB and at least 48 pixels wide. A PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best. Large images are scaled down for you.

Once a logo is added you can **Replace** it, or remove it with the small X and confirm with **Yes, remove**. A removed logo comes off every new image once you save.

**Show logo on images** turns the logo on or off for generated images. If it is on and you have not uploaded a logo, the identity's name is shown in its place.

## Brand Colours

| Colour                | What it is used for                                                  |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary colour**    | The dominant colour for diagram hubs, icons and badges.              |
| **Accent colour**     | Highlighted headline words, arrows and tags.                         |
| **Background colour** | The background behind the text. Use a soft white or cool light grey. |

Pick a colour with the swatch, or type a hex code such as #0E7C86. Next to a colour you see a plain name for it, such as "Reads as dark teal", so you can check it is what you meant. Click the X beside a colour to clear it. Without a primary colour, images use a standard blue; without an accent colour, they use the primary colour; without a background colour, they use a soft white.

The background must be light, because the headline and diagram are set on it. A dark colour is not accepted.

## Image Style

The image style decides how every new image is made:

| Style                       | What you get                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Like my posts** (default) | An image like the ones you already post on that platform: the same kind of image, layout, colours and typography. |
| **Photorealistic**          | A real-looking photograph of the post's world, lit and framed like editorial photography.                         |
| **Illustration**            | A clean drawn illustration in your brand colours, with labelled badges.                                           |
| **3D Render**               | A polished 3D scene with realistic materials and studio lighting.                                                 |
| **Minimal Graphic Design**  | A clean diagram, such as steps, layers, key points or a comparison, with exact labels.                            |

With **Like my posts**, your logo goes where your own posts keep theirs, and only when they show one; your colours are used where your posts leave a choice. In the other styles, a photograph or 3D render fills its side of the image to the edges, while an illustration sits on your background. Whatever the style, each image is still fitted to your industry, your audience and the post itself.

<Tags type="tip">

The style here is the default for the identity. You can pick a different style for a single image on the post itself, without changing the theme.

</Tags>

## Typography

Choose a **Headline typeface** and a **Body & label typeface** from the list. Each option says whether it is a sans or serif face. Both start as Inter.

## Save your theme

Changes are not used until you save them. Click **Save changes** at the top of the page; the button reads **Saved** when there is nothing new to save. **Discard** puts back what was last saved. If you try to leave the page with unsaved changes, your browser asks you to confirm.

After saving you see "Brand theme saved". New images for this identity use the theme from then on. Images that were already made keep the look they had.

<Tags type="note">

The background pattern and the website shown on images are not set here. You set them from a post's image settings on the Social Media Posts page.

</Tags>
