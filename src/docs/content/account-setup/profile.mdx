# Profile

The Profile says who is posting, what they post about, who it is for and where they can be found. Every idea and post is written from it.

## Edit and save your profile

A saved profile opens as plain text. To change it:

1. Click **Edit** in the header.
2. Change any field.
3. Click **Save**. You stay on the step; **Next** moves on to Voice.

**Cancel** puts back what was saved. If a required detail is missing, the page says so: click **Edit** to complete it.

The header also shows the setup steps (Profile, Voice, Knowledge, Preferences, Runs) and which one you are on.

## Profile fields

### Posting as

Choose **Me** (a person posting as themselves) or **A brand** (a business, product or page). For a brand, **Where You Work** becomes **Parent Company**. How posts sound on each platform is a separate choice, made in [Preferences](/docs/account-setup/preferences).

### Basic information

| Field                   | Required | Longest          | What to write                                                       |
| ----------------------- | -------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Name**                | Yes      | 200 characters   | The person's or brand's name                                        |
| **Where You Work**      | No       | 200 characters   | The company, or the parent company for a brand                      |
| **What You Post About** | Yes      | 200 characters   | For example "Bollywood dance, B2B sales, home baking"               |
| **About**               | Yes      | 5,000 characters | Who you are and what you post                                       |
| **Who It's For**        | Yes      | 2,000 characters | The people your posts are written for                               |
| **What You Promote**    | No       | 3,000 characters | Classes, a course, a product or services; leave it empty if nothing |

A counter appears as a field nears its limit and turns red when it is over. Saving with a required field empty shows "Please fill in all required fields marked with \*."

<Tags type="tip">

Be specific in **Who It's For**. The more precisely you describe the people you write for, the better your posts land with them.

</Tags>

### Online presence

There is a **profile** link field for each platform the identity posts on (for example **LinkedIn profile**), plus **Website URL**. All are optional.

- Links must start with http:// or https:// and be 500 characters or fewer.
- Each link must be on its own platform. A LinkedIn link in the X field shows "This is a LinkedIn link. Put your X profile here."
- Emptying a field and saving removes that link, and what was learned from it.

The fields shown are the platforms turned on in **Preferences → Platforms**, plus any platform that already has a saved link.

## Profile insights from your public posts

When you save a new or changed profile link for LinkedIn, X, Threads, Facebook or Instagram, the app reads your public posts there to learn how you post on that platform. From then on, every generation reads the latest version of what it learned, including your edits:

| Where                      | What it uses                                                                                 |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Suggest goals**          | What you post about and the kinds of post you make, on the platforms you post on.            |
| Research for a run         | This week's news on the two subjects you post about most, on the run's platforms.            |
| Ideas and their review     | What you post about, the kinds of post you make and your tone, so ideas fit your account.    |
| Posts on that platform     | What you post about, how you write, tone, captions and hashtags. New posts never copy yours. |
| Carousel slides and images | Slides follow your tone and writing; slides and images follow how your posts look there.     |

What was learned on one platform is only used for that platform's posts and slides: your LinkedIn style never shapes an Instagram caption.

The line under each link shows where things stand:

| What you see                                               | What it means                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Once saved, your public … posts are read"                 | You typed a link but have not saved it yet.                |
| "Reading your public … posts… this takes a minute or two." | The analysis is running. A message tells you when it ends. |
| "Learned from … posts", with **View** and **Refresh**      | The analysis is ready.                                     |
| "Not analyzed yet." with **Learn from my … posts**         | No analysis exists. Click to start one.                    |
| A warning with **Try again** and **Describe it yourself**  | Your posts could not be analyzed, with the reason.         |

### Read and correct what was learned

Click **View** to open **How you post on** that platform. It covers **In short**, **What you post about**, **How you write**, **Tone**, **Captions**, **Hashtags**, **How your posts look** (used for the images made for your posts there) and **Kinds of post**, with the posts it was learned from.

The posts come from public posts as search engines show them (often only the opening) and from posts you sent through Buffer. Nothing is read from the platform itself, and new posts never copy yours.

To correct it, click **Edit**, change any part, and click **Save**. Leave out anything you are not sure of. Analyzing again replaces your changes, so you are asked first.

If no posts could be found (the account may be private or new), click **Describe it yourself** and fill in the same parts by hand.

### Example posts for your images

Under each link, the images of your posts found on that profile are shown as small thumbnails. Images and carousel slides made in the **Like my posts** style (the [Brand Theme](/docs/account-setup/brand-theme#image-style) default) look like these: the same kind of image, layout, colours and type, with new words from each post.

Search engines often show only a few of an account's images. Click **Add images** to add your own: pick images you have posted on that platform, in JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 8 MB each and 12 per platform. Large images are made smaller before they are added. Point at a thumbnail and click its X to remove it.

| What you see                                         | What it means                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "New images follow these … posts."                   | Images for that platform's posts are made like these.               |
| "No … examples yet, so new … images follow your …"   | That platform borrows the examples of the platform that has most.   |
| "No example posts yet."                              | No platform has examples: images are made in the Photorealistic style. |

Images found on the profile are replaced each time it is analyzed again, and removed with the link. Images you added stay until you remove them.

## Fill in your profile with AI

The **AI Profile Assistant** sits beside the Profile step.

1. Paste your social media bio, company description or website content, or click **Use an example** to see what works.
2. Optionally add a **Main Profile Link** and **Website URL** for the analysis to use. Both start empty, and only the links you add are read; **Use saved links** fills in the ones on your profile. Typing them here does not change the links on your profile.
3. Click **Re-analyze**. It usually takes about a minute; **Cancel analysis** stops it. **Advanced analysis options** lets you set the identity type, voice, goal and topics for more accurate results.
4. When it finishes, the panel lists which of Profile, Voice, Knowledge, Goals and Preferences have suggestions. Click **View AI suggestions** to open **Review AI suggestions**.
5. Tick the changes you want. Anything you leave unchecked keeps its current value. **Edit Suggestions** lets you change a value first.
6. Click **Apply Selected Changes**. Applying never starts a run.

**Discard** throws the suggestions away without touching your saved settings. A dot on a step in the step bar means AI suggestions are waiting there.

### Switch an identity to another company

When the analysis describes a different company from the one the identity was set up for (say you rewrite a Zomato identity as TDCCommerce), the review opens as **Switch Zomato to TDCCommerce** instead. The same happens after you change the profile to another company by hand: the Profile page then says your voice, knowledge, goals and ideas were set up for the old company, and the next **Re-analyze** offers the switch. Until you switch, what was learned from links that do not look like the new company's is not used in ideas or posts, and the Profile page names those links.

An identity renamed before switching existed may still hold the old company's links. When a brand's saved link looks like another account's (for example instagram.com/zomato on TDCCommerce), the Profile page says so, and the review offers **Switch identity to …**.

A switch makes the identity the new company everywhere. Before you click **Switch to …**, the review lists what happens:

| What                                     | What a switch does                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Profile                                  | Every field takes the new value. A field the analysis left empty is cleared; a required one must be filled in first. |
| Profile links                            | Only ticked links are kept. A saved link that does not look like the new company's starts unticked and is removed.   |
| Voice, platform styles, guardrails       | Replaced. Settings not suggested go back to their defaults, and the style learned from the old posts is cleared.     |
| Goals                                    | The old goals are paused and kept. The new goal becomes the active one.                                              |
| Knowledge                                | The new company's pages are added. The old documents you tick are removed, with what was learned from them.          |
| Open ideas                               | Moved to Rejected, so none becomes a post for the new company.                                                       |
| Profile insights, newsletter suggestions | Cleared, except insights from a link that stays. Newsletters you saved or signed up to are kept.                     |
| Brand theme                              | Reset if you tick it.                                                                                                |
| Connected accounts                       | Stay connected, with auto-publish turned off until you check they belong to the new company.                         |
| Posts, published history, past runs      | Kept as they are.                                                                                                    |

From then on, new ideas and posts are written only from the new company's details: the old company's past posts, ideas and rejections no longer steer them.

If the switch fails, nothing is changed. A knowledge page that cannot be added stays in your AI suggestions to add again. A switch waits while a run or post writing is still working for the identity.

Click **Same company? Review as updates instead** if it is the same company under a new description. To switch when the review does not offer it, click **Switch identity instead**.

## When setup is complete

Once Voice, at least one Knowledge document, a goal and Preferences are all saved, the Profile page shows **Setup Complete — Ready to Generate!**, with links back to Voice, Knowledge and Preferences. Click **Generate Now** to go to Runs and start one.
