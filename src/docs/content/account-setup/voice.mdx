# Voice

Voice is how your posts sound: the writing style, who is speaking, how formal and direct the writing is, and how many emoji and hashtags it uses. Each identity has its own.

## Where your voice is used

Your voice is the identity's core voice on every platform. It shapes the ideas a run proposes and every draft: LinkedIn posts, carousel slides, and the versions written for X, Threads, Facebook and Instagram.

On platforms other than LinkedIn you can go further in [Preferences](/docs/account-setup/preferences): say how you sound there, and teach the app how you write there from a few of your own posts. Those add to your core voice for that platform. LinkedIn has no settings of its own there: it uses the core voice.

## Voice settings

All the settings sit in one section, **Adjust if needed**. Change anything that does not sound like you.

### Writing style

| Option           | What posts lean on                                  |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Candid**       | Honest opinions and hard-won lessons, said plainly. |
| **Storytelling** | Real moments and experiences that lead to a lesson. |
| **Analytical**   | Numbers, evidence and clear reasoning.              |
| **Educational**  | Practical tips and step-by-step advice.             |
| **Inspiring**    | Big-picture ideas that motivate the reader.         |

### Who is speaking

- **Me**: posts are written as "I".
- **My team**: posts are written as "we".
- **Brand**: posts are written in the third person.

This is separate from **Posting as** on your [Profile](/docs/account-setup/profile), which says whether the identity is a person or a brand.

### Formality

**Casual**, **Balanced** or **Formal**: from relaxed and conversational to formal.

### Directness

**Diplomatic**, **Balanced** or **Punchy**: from careful and tactful to blunt.

### Emoji

| Option         | What drafts do                                      |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **None**       | Use no emoji at all.                                |
| **As bullets** | Use emoji only as list markers, never mid-sentence. |
| **Freely**     | Use emoji where they add warmth.                    |

This setting is for LinkedIn and for platforms whose style is professional. Platforms set to casual or fun use emoji freely, unless you pick something else for that platform under **Preferences → Platforms**.

### Hashtags

**None**, **Up to 3** or **3 to 5**, placed at the end of the post. This applies to LinkedIn posts. On the other platforms, the number of hashtags follows what suits each platform.

## Save your voice

Click **Save** in the header. You see "New posts will be written in this voice."

- The first time, choose at least one setting. Saving with nothing chosen shows "Choose at least one voice setting before saving."
- Anything you leave unselected is saved with a default, and the page shows it once saved.
- Once saved, the page opens read-only. Click **Edit** to change it, and **Cancel** to put back what was saved.

New ideas and drafts use the saved voice. Posts already drafted keep their text.

## Suggestions from the AI setup

If you used the AI Profile Assistant or **Set it up with AI**, its voice suggestions fill in the settings you have not chosen. A banner says which fields the AI filled, and that these are recommended settings for your stated brand, not learned from your posts.

- **Undo** puts the form back as it was.
- **Use AI values for all** also replaces the settings you chose yourself.
- Nothing is saved until you click **Save**.

The AI setup can also write a short description of your writing style. Drafts follow it, but it is not shown or edited on this page.

<Tags type="tip" label="Want drafts to sound like your real posts?">

The Voice page does not read your posts. Two other places do:

- **Profile insights**: save your profile links on the [Profile](/docs/account-setup/profile) and your public posts are read to learn how you post on each platform.
- **Learn from your posts**: in [Preferences](/docs/account-setup/preferences), paste a few of your posts for any platform other than LinkedIn.
