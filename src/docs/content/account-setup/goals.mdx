# Goals

Goals say what your posts should achieve, and they steer every idea a run proposes.

<Eyebrow href="/goals">Goals</Eyebrow>

## See your goals in context

### Add a goal

Click **New Goal** to say what your posts should achieve. Each goal card shows its objective and key message.

## How goals steer ideas

A run works toward the goals you pick for it, and only those. It researches them, looks them up in your knowledge base and writes each idea to serve one of them. Each idea is then scored on whether it reaches the right readers, lands the goal's key message and gives readers a reason to take the goal's action. Ideas that miss their goal are dropped before you see them.

An idea's main goal sets its call to action. An idea can also move another goal of the same run; its card then shows both, the main goal first.

## Create a goal

Click **New Goal**. The form opens by itself when you have no goals yet.

| Field                         | What to enter                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Goal Title** (required)     | A short name you will recognise when picking goals for a run. Each goal of an identity needs a different title. |
| **Objective**                 | What the goal sets out to do. See the list below.                                                               |
| **Posts on**                  | Shown when you post on more than one platform. See below.                                                       |
| **Key Message** (required)    | The one point you want readers to come round to.                                                                |
| **Call to Action (Optional)** | What you want readers to do, such as "Comment with your experience".                                            |
| **Link to Share (Optional)**  | A page your posts should link to.                                                                               |
| **Links Go In**               | Appears once you add a link: **First comment (better reach)** or **Post body**.                                 |

Click **Save Goal →**. A goal cannot be saved without a title and key message, or with a title another goal already uses.

<Tags type="tip">

Make the **Key Message** specific. "Most teams fix this problem with more tools; the real fix is a simpler process." gives a run far more to work with than "We are experts".

</Tags>

### Objectives you can pick

The objectives offered depend on how you post on the goal's platforms (all your platforms, for a goal on **All platforms**). Each platform is set to Professional, Casual or Fun in Preferences; LinkedIn starts as Professional and the others as Casual.

| Objective                                              | Offered for              |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Authority (Thought leadership & POV)                   | Professional platforms   |
| Awareness (Reach & impressions)                        | Both                     |
| Engagement (Comments, shares & DMs)                    | Both                     |
| Lead gen (Inbound inquiries & demos)                   | Professional platforms   |
| Recruiting (Attracting talent)                         | Professional platforms   |
| Community (Building a loyal following)                 | Both                     |
| Grow followers (New people find and follow you)        | Casual and fun platforms |
| Entertain (Make people laugh, smile or feel something) | Casual and fun platforms |
| Promote (A class, event, product or launch)            | Casual and fun platforms |
| Collabs (Attract brands and other creators)            | Casual and fun platforms |

If the goal's platforms are a mix, you get both lists. The first objective in the list is picked by default.

### Limit a goal to some platforms

Under **Posts on**, leave **All platforms** selected, or pick only the platforms this goal is for. With platforms picked, ideas for the goal go only on those platforms, and runs planned for another platform leave the goal out. Picking every platform is the same as **All platforms**. A limited goal's card shows a tag such as "LinkedIn only".

## Get AI goal suggestions

Click **Suggest Goals** to have the AI suggest up to three goals based on your saved profile, under **Suggested Goals**. Click **Use This Goal** to copy one into the New Goal form; nothing is saved until you review it and click **Save Goal →**.

Suggestions need offerings and an audience in your profile to build on. They only ever link to pages you saved yourself, such as your website, and their cost counts under **Identity & goals**.

If you used AI setup on your profile, the New Goal form may open already filled in with a suggested goal. Saving it creates a new goal; your existing goals are not changed.

## Edit or delete a goal

Click the pencil icon on a goal to edit it. The form changes to **Edit Goal** and the list is hidden while you work. Click **Update Goal →** to save, or **Cancel**.

Click the bin icon to delete a goal, then **Delete** to confirm. Deleting is permanent. Ideas already made for the goal keep its message, so their posts still land it.

## See what each goal has produced

The bottom of each goal card shows what it has produced, for example "4 ideas · 2 posts", or "No ideas yet". An idea counts toward every goal it serves, not only its main one, and posts are those written from those ideas. A goal still at "No ideas yet" after several runs is worth a closer look.

## Generate ideas from this page

You can start a run without leaving Goals:

- **Generate ideas** on a goal's card starts a run for that goal only, straight away, and opens the run's page.
- **Generate ideas** at the top opens the run dialog with every active goal ticked.
- Tick goals to pick several (**Select all active** ticks them all; **Clear** unticks them). The top button then reads, for example, **Generate ideas (2)**, and the dialog lists only those goals.

A paused goal is shown faded. It cannot be ticked, has no **Generate ideas** button and is never used in a run.

### The bottom action bar

While goals are ticked, a bar floats at the bottom of the page with the number selected, **Clear**, and **Generate ideas for 2 goals** (with your count), which opens the run dialog for exactly those goals. It is hidden while you edit a goal.
