# Quick Start

This guide takes you from a new account to your first published post. Each step links to a page with the details, so you can follow it straight through or come back to one step later.

The app walks you through the same journey itself. The **Getting started** checklist on the [Dashboard](/docs/getting-started/dashboard) shows every step, ticks each one off as soon as its work is saved, and always shows what to do next. Until your first post is out, a guide bar at the top of every page does the same. Progress comes from what you have saved, so you can stop at any point and pick up where you left off.

## 1. Sign in

On the sign-in page, choose **Continue with Google** or **Sign in with a passkey**. There is no password to create. See [Security](/docs/settings/security) to add a passkey later.

## 2. Create your identity

An identity is the person or brand the engine writes for.

1. In the sidebar, click **Set up identity**.
2. **Where you post**: pick every platform the identity posts on, then click **Continue**.
3. **About you**: describe who it is, what it posts about and who it is for. Add its profile links and website if you have them.
4. Click **Set it up with AI** to have the profile and each platform's style filled in for you, or **I'll fill it in myself**.
5. **Review** what was filled in, then click **Create identity**.

Nothing is saved to your account until you click **Create identity**, but what you type is kept in your browser: leave half way and you come back to it, with **Start over** if you would rather begin again.

Writing for more than one person or brand? Each gets an identity of its own: see [Identities](/docs/getting-started/identities).

## 3. Finish the account setup

Three more setup steps tell the engine how the identity sounds and what it knows. The **Setup checklist** on the [Dashboard](/docs/getting-started/dashboard) shows what is still missing.

- [Voice](/docs/account-setup/voice): who is speaking, how formal and direct the posts are, emoji and hashtags.
- [Knowledge](/docs/account-setup/knowledge): paste the address of an article or case study and click **Fetch**, so posts are grounded in facts you stand behind.
- [Preferences](/docs/account-setup/preferences): **Ideas per run** (5 unless you change it), each platform's style, and topics to stay away from.

## 4. Set a goal

Goals say what your posts are for. A run needs at least one.

1. Open [Goals](/goals) and click **New Goal**.
2. Give it a **Goal Title**, pick an **Objective** and write the **Key Message**: the one point readers should come round to.
3. Click **Save Goal →**.

/>

Not sure where to start? **Suggest Goals** proposes a few for you to edit. See [Goals](/docs/account-setup/goals).

## 5. Start a run

1. On [Goals](/goals), click **Generate ideas**, or **Generate Now** on the Runs step.
2. Tick the goals the run should work toward and, if the identity posts on several platforms, choose under **Ideas for**.
3. Click **Generate for 1 goal** (the number follows your ticks).

<Screenshot
  name="generate-ideas"
  alt="The goal ticks and the generate button in the run dialog, boxed in red"
/>

The run's page opens and updates as the engine gathers context, researches the web, your knowledge and your newsletters, and writes ideas. It usually takes a few minutes, and you can leave the page meanwhile. When the status reads **Awaiting Idea Approval**, the ideas are ready. See [Starting a Run](/docs/account-setup/runs).

## 6. Review the ideas

Open [Ideas](/ideas); the number next to it in the sidebar counts ideas waiting for you. Each idea has a scorecard explaining how well it fits your goal, how original it is and how good its sources are.

- **Approve** the ones worth writing about. Approving does not write a post yet.
- **Reject** the rest. Later runs will not propose them again.

<Screenshot
  name="ideas"
  alt="An idea card with its Approve and Reject buttons boxed in red"
/>

See [Ideas](/docs/account-setup/ideas).

## 7. Create posts

1. Select the approved ideas and click **Create N posts**.
2. Pick **1. Platform** (one or more) and **2. Type of post**, such as a text post, an image post or a carousel. **Add an image** is ticked by default.
3. Confirm. The posts are drafted in your voice and arrive on [Social Media Posts](/posts) as **Awaiting review**.

<Screenshot
  name="create-posts"
  alt="The Create posts dialog with Platform and Type of post boxed in red"
/>

See [Overview](/docs/publishing/posts/overview) and [Post Types & Images](/docs/publishing/posts/post-types).

## 8. Edit and approve a post

Open a post to edit its text and image, check the warnings it shows, and preview it as it will appear. When it is ready, click **Approve** (or press <Kbd>A</Kbd>). See [Editing a Post](/docs/publishing/posts/editing).

<Screenshot
  name="post-editor"
  alt="A post's page with Edit text, Regenerate and Approve boxed in red"
/>

## 9. Publish it

Posts are published through your own Buffer account.

1. Click **Connect Buffer**, on the [Integrations](/settings/integrations) page or in the getting-started guide. After you allow access on Buffer's screen, you come back to the page you started from.
2. Check which account each network publishes to, and click **Confirm channels** in the guide. See [Integrations](/docs/settings/integrations).
3. On the approved post, click **Publish now**, or **Schedule** to pick a date and time.
4. Your first post opens a final check: the exact text, what goes with it, the account it is published as and when. Tick **I have read this post and want it published publicly** and confirm.

<Screenshot
  name="integrations"
  alt="The Connect Buffer button on Integrations, boxed in red"
/>

You can also turn on auto-publishing to send approved posts out on the day you plan them on the [Schedule](/docs/publishing/schedule), or copy a post and publish it yourself.

<Tags type="important" label="Nothing goes out without you">

Ideas wait for your approval, posts wait for your review, and only approved posts can be published, through accounts you connected yourself.

</Tags>

## Keep the ideas fresh

Newsletters give the engine new material for every run. On [Discover](/docs/publishing/newsletters/discover), find newsletters on your topics and sign up to them with the identity's own inbox address. Their stories then feed your runs. See [Inbox](/docs/publishing/newsletters/inbox).
