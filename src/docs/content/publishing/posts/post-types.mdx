# Post Types & Images

Every post has two choices behind it: the type of post for its platform (a LinkedIn post, an X thread…) and what goes out with the text (nothing, one image, or a carousel of slides). This page covers both, how the engine designs images in your brand, and short videos for Reels and Shorts.

## Choose a post type in context

### Pick what goes out with the text

The post type picker offers **Text only**, **Single image** or **Carousel**, as the platform allows.

## Types of post per platform

You pick the type of post under **2. Type of post** when you create posts from ideas (see [Overview](/docs/publishing/posts/overview)).

| Platform  | Type of post       | Text                                    | Can go out as                                                    |
| --------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| LinkedIn  | **LinkedIn post**  | Up to 3,000 characters                  | **Text only**, **Single image** or **Carousel** (2 to 20 slides) |
| X         | **X post**         | Up to 280 characters                    | **Text only** or **Single image**                                |
| X         | **X thread**       | 3 to 7 posts, each up to 280 characters | **Text only** or **Single image**                                |
| Threads   | **Threads post**   | Up to 500 characters                    | **Text only** or **Single image**                                |
| Facebook  | **Facebook post**  | Up to 2,000 characters                  | **Text only**, **Single image** or **Carousel** (2 to 10 slides) |
| Instagram | **Instagram post** | A caption of up to 2,200 characters     | **Single image** or **Carousel** (2 to 10 slides)                |

On X, every link counts as 23 characters and every emoji as 2.

Short videos are a separate choice, **Short video**, in the same dialog. See [Short videos](#short-videos) below.

| Platform  | Type of post       | Caption                                            | Goes out as                |
| --------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Instagram | **Instagram Reel** | Up to 2,200 characters                             | A video of the script, 9:16 |
| YouTube   | **YouTube Short**  | A title of up to 100 characters, and a description | A video of the script, 9:16 |
| Facebook  | **Facebook Reel**  | Up to 2,000 characters                             | A video of the script, 9:16 |

## Choose what goes out with the text

On a post's page, the post type picker above the image panel offers the choices its platform takes:

- **Text only**: the caption goes out on its own.
- **Single image**: the caption with one image.
- **Carousel**: the caption with slides to swipe through.

Instagram posts always need an image, so they have no **Text only**. Until you pick, a post goes out with its image if it has one, and as text only if it doesn't. Switching to **Text only** keeps the image aside: pick **Single image** to use it again. Once a post is published, its type is locked.

If the idea was planned as a carousel, or the caption tells readers to swipe, the panel says **The AI planned this post as a carousel**. Click **Make it a carousel**, then **Make slides**.

## Image sizes per platform

Every image the engine designs is made in the shape its platform shows best:

| Platform              | Shape             | Size in pixels |
| --------------------- | ----------------- | -------------- |
| LinkedIn              | Landscape, 1.91:1 | 2048 × 1072    |
| X                     | Landscape, 16:9   | 2048 × 1152    |
| Facebook              | Landscape, 1.91:1 | 2048 × 1072    |
| Instagram and Threads | Portrait, 4:5     | 1536 × 1920    |

Carousel slides are portrait, 1080 × 1350, on every platform. An image made from a reference you attached takes the feed shape closest to that reference instead.

If a post's image is another shape (made for another platform, a stock photo, or an image from a link), a notice says it may be cropped. Click **Regenerate image** to get one sized for the platform.

## Image styles

| Style                      | What you get                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Like my posts**          | An image like the ones you already post on that platform: the same kind of image, layout, colours and typography. |
| **Photorealistic**         | A real-looking photograph of the post's world, lit and framed like editorial photography.                         |
| **Illustration**           | A clean drawn illustration in your brand colours, with labelled badges.                                           |
| **3D Render**              | A polished 3D scene with realistic materials and studio lighting.                                                 |
| **Minimal Graphic Design** | A clean diagram (steps, layers, key points or a comparison) with exact labels.                                    |

Your [Brand Theme](/brand) sets the default, which is **Like my posts** unless you change it. To use another style for one image, pick it under **Style** in the image panel before you generate. The link beside it, for example **Back to the Brand Theme's Like my posts**, returns to the default.

**Like my posts** follows the example posts for the post's platform on the [Profile](/docs/account-setup/profile#example-posts-for-your-images) page, or another platform's when that one has none. Until the identity has example posts, it makes a **Photorealistic** image. With a prompt of your own, a few example posts go along as the look to follow.

With **Minimal Graphic Design**, open **Image options** to choose a **Diagram type**: **Auto** (the engine picks), **Hub & spoke**, **Steps**, **Layers**, **Key points** or **Comparison**. An image made from a reference, or an edit of the current one, keeps that image's own look.

## How images are made

If **Add an image** was ticked when the post was created, the engine designs its image while drafting. If that fails, the image panel says why, and you can generate one yourself (see [Editing a Post](/docs/publishing/posts/editing)).

With **Like my posts**, the engine picks the one to three example posts that suit this post, writes the words for the image from the post, and the image is made to look like those examples. It is then read back: every word is compared with what was asked for, and invented figures, other brands' logos, anything copied from the examples and flaws that look generated are listed under **Check this image before posting**. An image with problems is made once more, and the better of the two is kept.

In the other styles, each designed image combines a picture in the chosen style (or a diagram) with short text in your brand's typefaces and colours. In the picture styles, the picture is checked for stray lettering and other flaws, and anything found is listed under **Check this image before posting**.

## Your logo, colours and website

Every image the engine designs uses your [Brand Theme](/brand)'s logo and colours; the image panel says which it has. Under **Image options** you can also set the **Background** (**Auto**, **Dots**, **Grid** or **Plain**) and the **Website** shown on images (up to 60 characters). Both are saved for the identity and used for the next images.

## Carousels

### Make the slides

Pick **Carousel**, then click **Make slides**. The AI writes a cover, slides with one point each and a closing call to action, beside your caption rather than repeating it. A new carousel has 6 to 9 slides on LinkedIn, 4 to 6 on Facebook and 5 to 8 on Instagram.

How the slides look depends on your Brand Theme's image style:

| Image style                                                             | How the slides are made                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Like my posts**, with [example posts](/docs/account-setup/profile#example-posts-for-your-images) | Each slide is one image made like the posts you already publish on that platform, with the slide's words set into it. The cover is made first and the other slides are made to match it, so they read as one set. Takes two to three minutes. |
| Any other style, or no example posts yet                                | The slides are drawn in your Brand Theme's colours, typefaces and logo. In a picture style, the cover gets a picture.                                                                                                                                      |

Each slide made like your posts is read back like a single image: every word is compared with the slide's words, and a slide with a wrong word is made once more. Anything still wrong shows under the slide.

### Edit slides

Each slide is labelled **Cover**, **Call to action**, or by its layout, and has these buttons:

- **Edit this slide**: its **Headline**, its text, its **Layout** (**Statement**, **Icon**, **Big number**, **List**, **Quote** or **Photo**) and its picture prompt. Saving redraws it.
- **Write this slide again with AI**, with an optional note such as "shorter".
- **Add a picture** (or **New picture**) and **Remove the picture**.
- **Move up**, **Move down** and **Delete this slide**. The cover and the last slide stay where they are.

**Add a slide after slide N** adds one, up to the platform's maximum.

A slide made like your posts is one image with its words in it, so it works a little differently:

- **Edit this slide** changes its **Headline**, its smaller line (or a few points, one per line) and what it shows beside its words. **Save and make again** makes the slide again with the new words and keeps its design. This takes about half a minute.
- **Make this slide again** gives another version with the same words, made to match the cover.
- It has no layout and no separate picture, so those controls are not shown.
- A slide you add says **Not made** until you edit its words or write it with AI.

### Work on the whole carousel

- **Write again**, then **Write slides**: replaces every slide and your edits to them. The caption stays.
- **Redraw**: draws every slide again with your current Brand Theme, without AI. Use it after changing your logo or colours. Slides made like your posts stay as they are, so a carousel made only of those has no **Redraw**.
- **Images** and **PDF**: download the slides.

A published carousel goes out as one post with all its slides as images.

## Short videos

The engine can turn an idea into a short vertical video for **Instagram Reels**, **YouTube Shorts** or **Facebook Reels**. Each video plays the script as slides: the opening line on a title card, each shot's few words in big type with its line as a caption under them, and the call to action on a closing card with your logo and website or handle. It is recorded on the server: 9:16 (1080 × 1920), MP4, usually 10 to 30 seconds, with no voiceover or music, so the words on screen carry it.

### How a video looks

A video is made to look like your own posts:

- **Your [Brand Theme](/docs/account-setup/brand-theme) comes first.** Its colours, typefaces, logo, website or handle and background pattern are used as you set them. An image style set there also decides: **Minimal Graphic Design** gives light, quiet slides, and **Photorealistic** puts your photos behind the words.
- **What it leaves unset follows your posts.** The engine reads what it learned from your public profile on that platform (see [Profile](/docs/account-setup/profile)) and a few of your post images, and picks a look to match: clean cards, bold dark slides, light minimal slides, or your own photos behind the words; plus colours, capitals, caption style and pace. A YouTube Short follows your Instagram, else your Facebook, because YouTube channels are not studied.
- With nothing learned and no Brand Theme, a video uses the default look.

Matching your posts takes one small AI call (about a cent) the first time for each platform, charged to that post. Later videos reuse the look until your profile is analyzed again, your example images change, or you change your brand colours. Recording itself costs nothing.

### Create a video post

1. On [Ideas](/ideas), select ideas and click **Create N posts**.
2. In the dialog, switch from **Image post** to **Short video**.
3. Pick one or more platforms. You don't need to turn these platforms on in Preferences first.
4. Confirm. Each idea gets a script for every platform you picked: three to six shots, each with a few words for its slide and a line for its caption, plus a caption and hashtags, and a title for a Short. Video posts get no image.

The video starts on its own as soon as the script is written and is recorded in about a minute: right away when you open the post, or on the next background pass. You don't need to keep any page open.

Reels and Shorts are only made this way. **Add a version** and runs never write them.

### The video on the post page

A video post's page shows a video card where the image panel would be:

- **Recording your video…** with the time elapsed while it is made. You can leave the page; it keeps going.
- Once it is ready, the video plays in the card, with **Download MP4**.
- **Record again** records the script as it is now. Your current video stays until the new one is ready.
- If the video could not be recorded, the card says why and offers **Try again**. If none was started, click **Record video**.

The video shows the script's words. To change what it says, edit the post text and save, then click **Record again**. To change its colours, typefaces or logo, update your Brand Theme and record it again. Once the post is published, its video can no longer change.

### Publish a video

Approve the post, then **Publish now** or **Schedule** as usual. The caption and hashtags go out with the video, and a YouTube Short gets its title. Reels and Shorts can only be published once their video is ready, and auto-publishing waits for it too. You can also **Download MP4** and post it yourself.

Every video is marked as AI-generated where the platform supports it.
