# Post Types & Images

Every post has two choices behind it: the type of post for its platform (a LinkedIn post, an X thread…) and what goes out with the text (nothing, one image, or a carousel of slides). This page covers both, how the engine designs images in your brand, and short videos for Reels and Shorts.

## Types of post per platform

You pick the type of post under **2. Type of post** when you create posts from ideas (see [Overview](/docs/publishing/posts/overview)).

| Platform  | Type of post       | Text                                    | Can go out as                                                    |
| --------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| LinkedIn  | **LinkedIn post**  | Up to 3,000 characters                  | **Text only**, **Single image** or **Carousel** (2 to 20 slides) |
| X         | **X post**         | Up to 280 characters                    | **Text only** or **Single image**                                |
| X         | **X thread**       | 3 to 7 posts, each up to 280 characters | **Text only** or **Single image**                                |
| Threads   | **Threads post**   | Up to 500 characters                    | **Text only** or **Single image**                                |
| Facebook  | **Facebook post**  | Up to 2,000 characters                  | **Text only**, **Single image** or **Carousel** (2 to 10 slides) |
| Instagram | **Instagram post** | A caption of up to 2,200 characters     | **Single image** or **Carousel** (2 to 10 slides)                |

On X, every link counts as 23 characters and every emoji as 2.

Short videos are a separate choice, **Short video**, in the same dialog. See [Short videos](#short-videos) below.

| Platform  | Type of post       | Caption                                            | Goes out as                |
| --------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Instagram | **Instagram Reel** | Up to 2,200 characters                             | An 8-second AI video, 9:16 |
| YouTube   | **YouTube Short**  | A title of up to 100 characters, and a description | An 8-second AI video, 9:16 |
| Facebook  | **Facebook Reel**  | Up to 2,000 characters                             | An 8-second AI video, 9:16 |

## Choose what goes out with the text

On a post's page, the post type picker above the image panel offers the choices its platform takes:

- **Text only**: the caption goes out on its own.
- **Single image**: the caption with one image.
- **Carousel**: the caption with slides to swipe through.

Instagram posts always need an image, so they have no **Text only**. Until you pick, a post goes out with its image if it has one, and as text only if it doesn't. Switching to **Text only** keeps the image aside: pick **Single image** to use it again. Once a post is published, its type is locked.

If the idea was planned as a carousel, or the caption tells readers to swipe, the panel says **The AI planned this post as a carousel**. Click **Make it a carousel**, then **Make slides**.

## Image sizes per platform

Every image the engine designs is made in the shape its platform shows best:

| Platform              | Shape             | Size in pixels |
| --------------------- | ----------------- | -------------- |
| LinkedIn              | Landscape, 1.91:1 | 2048 × 1072    |
| X                     | Landscape, 16:9   | 2048 × 1152    |
| Facebook              | Landscape, 1.91:1 | 2048 × 1072    |
| Instagram and Threads | Portrait, 4:5     | 1536 × 1920    |

Carousel slides are portrait, 1080 × 1350, on every platform. An image made from a reference you attached takes the feed shape closest to that reference instead.

If a post's image is another shape (made for another platform, a stock photo, or an image from a link), a notice says it may be cropped. Click **Regenerate image** to get one sized for the platform.

## Image styles

| Style                      | What you get                                                                              |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Photorealistic**         | A real-looking photograph of the post's world, lit and framed like editorial photography. |
| **Illustration**           | A clean drawn illustration in your brand colours, with labelled badges.                   |
| **3D Render**              | A polished 3D scene with realistic materials and studio lighting.                         |
| **Minimal Graphic Design** | A clean diagram (steps, layers, key points or a comparison) with exact labels.            |

Your [Brand Theme](/brand) sets the default, which is **Photorealistic** unless you change it. To use another style for one image, pick it under **Style** in the image panel before you generate. The link beside it, for example **Back to the Brand Theme's Photorealistic**, returns to the default.

With **Minimal Graphic Design**, open **Image options** to choose a **Diagram type**: **Auto** (the engine picks), **Hub & spoke**, **Steps**, **Layers**, **Key points** or **Comparison**. An image made from a reference, or an edit of the current one, keeps that image's own look.

## How images are made

If **Add an image** was ticked when the post was created, the engine designs its image while drafting. If that fails, the image panel says why, and you can generate one yourself (see [Editing a Post](/docs/publishing/posts/editing)).

Each designed image combines a picture in the chosen style (or a diagram) with short text in your brand's typefaces and colours. In the picture styles, the picture is checked for stray lettering and other flaws, and anything found is listed under **Check this image before posting**.

## Your logo, colours and website

Every image the engine designs uses your [Brand Theme](/brand)'s logo and colours; the image panel says which it has. Under **Image options** you can also set the **Background** (**Auto**, **Dots**, **Grid** or **Plain**) and the **Website** shown on images (up to 60 characters). Both are saved for the identity and used for the next images.

## Carousels

### Make the slides

Pick **Carousel**, then click **Make slides**. The AI writes a cover, slides with one point each and a closing call to action, beside your caption rather than repeating it, and draws them in your brand. A new carousel has 6 to 9 slides on LinkedIn, 4 to 6 on Facebook and 5 to 8 on Instagram. If your Brand Theme uses a picture style, the cover gets a picture.

### Edit slides

Each slide is labelled **Cover**, **Call to action**, or by its layout, and has these buttons:

- **Edit this slide**: its **Headline**, its text, its **Layout** (**Statement**, **Icon**, **Big number**, **List**, **Quote** or **Photo**) and its picture prompt. Saving redraws it.
- **Write this slide again with AI**, with an optional note such as "shorter".
- **Add a picture** (or **New picture**) and **Remove the picture**.
- **Move up**, **Move down** and **Delete this slide**. The cover and the last slide stay where they are.

**Add a slide after slide N** adds one, up to the platform's maximum.

### Work on the whole carousel

- **Write again**, then **Write slides**: replaces every slide and your edits to them. The caption stays.
- **Redraw**: draws every slide again with your current Brand Theme, without AI. Use it after changing your logo or colours.
- **Images** and **PDF**: download the slides.

A published carousel goes out as one post with all its slides as images.

## Short videos

The engine can turn an idea into a short vertical video for **Instagram Reels**, **YouTube Shorts** or **Facebook Reels**. Each is one AI-generated clip: 8 seconds, 9:16 (1080 × 1920), MP4, with a voiceover.

### Create a video post

1. On [Ideas](/ideas), select ideas and click **Create N posts**.
2. In the dialog, switch from **Image post** to **Short video**.
3. Pick one or more platforms. The dialog shows the estimated cost of each video, for example "About $0.64 per video". You don't need to turn these platforms on in Preferences first.
4. Confirm. Each idea gets a script for every platform you picked: one to three shots with the words spoken over them, plus a caption and hashtags, and a title for a Short. Video posts get no image.

The video starts on its own as soon as the script is written, and takes about 1 to 3 minutes. It is made in the background, so you don't need to keep any page open.

Reels and Shorts are only made this way. **Add a version** and runs never write them.

### The video on the post page

A video post's page shows a video card where the image panel would be:

- **Generating your video…** with the time elapsed while it is made. You can leave the page; it keeps going.
- Once it is ready, the video plays in the card, with **Download MP4**.
- **Regenerate video** makes a new one. Type what should change under **What should change? (optional)**, for example "a brighter office setting, a calmer voice", check the estimated cost, then click **Generate**. Your current video stays until the new one is ready.
- If the video could not be made, the card says why and offers **Try again**. If none was started (for example, a spending limit was reached while drafting), click **Generate video**.

The video follows the script's spoken words. To change what it says, edit the post text and save, then regenerate the video. Each video counts toward your [AI spending limits](/docs/help/ai-usage). Once the post is published, its video can no longer change.

### Publish a video

Approve the post, then **Publish now** or **Schedule** as usual. The caption and hashtags go out with the video, and a YouTube Short gets its title. Reels and Shorts can only be published once their video is ready, and auto-publishing waits for it too. You can also **Download MP4** and post it yourself.

Every video is marked as AI-generated where the platform supports it.
