# Overview

Posts lists every post drafted for the active identity, on every platform. Here you find what is waiting for review, what is approved, and what is already out.

## Where posts come from

Posts are drafted from ideas, never from scratch:

1. On [Ideas](/ideas), select the ideas you want to turn into posts.
2. Click **Create N posts**, or **Approve & Create Posts** if your selection includes ideas still waiting for review.
3. In the dialog, pick **1. Platform** (one or more) and **2. Type of post** for each platform, such as **X post** or **X thread**. **Add an image** is ticked by default. Instagram posts always get one.
4. Confirm. Each idea gets a post on every platform you picked, and you are taken to Posts.

You can do the same from a run's page in [Run Details](/runs). The **Create Post** button on Posts takes you to Ideas, and **From a link** writes a post like a public post, reel or video you paste the link to (see [Ideas](/docs/account-setup/ideas#create-a-post-like-one-you-saw)). Only the platforms turned on for the identity in [Preferences](/identity/preferences) are offered.

New posts arrive as **Awaiting review**. Nothing is published until you approve a post and publish or schedule it.

### Versions for other platforms

One idea can have a post on each platform, for example a LinkedIn post and an X thread. Each is its own post, reviewed and published separately. On a post's page, the **Versions** row switches between them, and **Add a version** writes the idea for another platform you have turned on.

## Find your way around the list

- **Platform tabs**: **All platforms**, or one platform's posts. Each tab shows how many posts it holds.
- **Status chips**: **All**, **Awaiting review**, **Approved**, **Published**, **Rejected** and **Drafts**. Every post is under exactly one chip. **Approved** includes posts that are scheduled or being published.
- **Search posts...**: searches the text and hashtags. Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>K</Kbd> (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd> + <Kbd>K</Kbd> on a Mac) to jump to it.
- **Filters**: a **Date range** (**From** and **To**) and **Hidden posts**. Click **Apply** to use them.
- **Sort**: **Newest first** or **Oldest first**.
- **Cards** or **List** view.

Posts are grouped by the day they were drafted. Older posts load in batches: click **Load older posts** at the end of the list. Search and filters only cover the posts loaded so far.

Your filters are kept in the page address, so **All posts** on a post's page brings you back to the list as you left it.

## Post statuses

Each post shows a coloured status:

| Status              | Meaning                                                                                    |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Awaiting review** | Drafted and waiting for you to approve or reject it.                                       |
| **Approved**        | Approved, but not scheduled or published yet.                                              |
| **Scheduled**       | Set to publish at a set time, or waiting for an automatic retry.                           |
| **Publishing**      | Being sent to the platform right now.                                                      |
| **Published**       | Out on its platform, or marked as posted on the Schedule page.                             |
| **Publish failed**  | The last attempt to publish did not work.                                                  |
| **Rejected**        | You rejected it. You can still approve it later.                                           |
| **Draft**           | Listed under **Drafts**. New posts from ideas start as **Awaiting review**, not as drafts. |

## What each post card offers

The button at the bottom of each card is the next step:

| Button            | When                                                                                    |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Review Draft**  | The post is awaiting review.                                                            |
| **Publish Now**   | It is approved and the app can publish it. Opens the post.                              |
| **Copy & post**   | It is approved, but the app doesn't publish this type of post, so you post it yourself. |
| **Retry publish** | The last attempt failed.                                                                |
| **Scheduled**     | It is set to go out. Hover to see when.                                                 |
| **Live on …**     | It is published. Click to open it on the platform.                                      |
| **Revisit**       | It was rejected.                                                                        |
| **Continue Edit** | It is a draft.                                                                          |

The ⋮ menu on each card has **Open post**, **Copy text**, **Hide** (or **Unhide**) and **Delete**.

- **Hide** only takes the post out of the list. Turn on **Hidden posts** in **Filters** to see it again. A hidden post that is scheduled still goes out.
- **Delete** removes the post from the app for good, with its images and alternates. Scheduled publishing is cancelled, and anything already published stays on its platform.

## The life of a post

1. **Drafted** from an idea: **Awaiting review**.
2. **Reviewed** on its page: you check the preview, the image and the checks, and edit anything you like. See [Editing a Post](/docs/publishing/posts/editing).
3. **Approved**, or **Rejected** with an optional reason that shapes future drafts.
4. **Published** with **Publish now**, at a set time with **Schedule**, automatically on its planned day, or by copying it and posting it yourself. See [Schedule](/docs/publishing/schedule).
5. **Live**: once it is out, its text and image can no longer change.
