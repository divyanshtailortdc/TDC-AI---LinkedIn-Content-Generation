# Editing a Post

Each post has its own page, where you review it, change its text and image, check it is ready, and approve it.

## The post page at a glance

- **Top bar**: **All posts** takes you back to the list as you left it. On the right are the post's status (hover over it for the next step), the review or publishing buttons, and the bin to delete the post.
- **Versions**: the same idea's posts on other platforms. Click one to switch, or **Add a version** to write the idea for another platform you have turned on.
- **Left**: a preview of the post on its platform, with **Copy text**, **Regenerate** and **Edit Post Text**. Below it are the **Ready for…** checks, **Earlier versions** and **AI cost for this post**.
- **Right**: the post type and the image panel, or for a Reel or a Short, its video. See [Post Types & Images](/docs/publishing/posts/post-types).

## Edit the text

1. Click **Edit Post Text**.
2. Change the **Opening Hook** and the **Body**. For an X thread these are **First post** and **Other posts, separated by a line with ---**.
3. Change the comment field if you like. It is called **First comment** on LinkedIn and Facebook, **Reply** on X and Threads, and **Link-in-bio note** on Instagram (a note for you, never posted).
4. Click **Save Changes**, or **Cancel** to drop your edits.

**View Feed Preview** shows the preview again without losing your edits, but they stay unsaved. You can't approve or publish while edits are unsaved.

### Swap the hook

In a LinkedIn preview, click **Swap hook** beside the opening line. **Original** is the AI's first opening, and **Alternate 1**, **Alternate 2** and so on are the others it wrote. Picking one edits the post.

### Rewrite with AI

Click **Regenerate**, say what should change under **What should change? (optional)** (for example "Shorter. Less salesy."), and click **Write new version**. Leave it empty for a different take on the same idea. The image stays as it is, and approving and publishing wait until the new text is in.

### Earlier versions

Every save, regenerate or restore keeps the text it replaced. **Earlier versions** lists them, with when and why each was replaced. Click **Restore** to bring one back; the text it replaces is kept too.

## Check the post is ready

Under the preview, **Ready for LinkedIn?** (or the post's platform) checks the post:

- A bar shows its length against the platform's limit, the length that reads best, and where the feed folds.
- Each check is fine, worth a look, or to fix. The summary reads **Ready to publish**, **N to look at** or **N to fix before publishing**.

The checks cover length, the opening line, hashtags, links (Instagram captions can't have clickable links), the image or slides, and words like "swipe" in a post that isn't a carousel. **Adapted** warns when the post reads almost the same as the idea's post on another platform; **Regenerate** rewrites it.

Some problems are refused at publish time too: a post over the platform's limit, an Instagram post without an image, or a carousel with the wrong number of slides or slides that couldn't be drawn. An X post, or any post of an X thread, over 280 characters can't even be approved.

## Change the image

- **Generate image** (or **Regenerate image**): with nothing typed in **Describe the image you want**, a new image is designed from the post. With a description, it follows your words instead of the post.
- **Design from the post text instead**: designs from the post, using what you typed as direction.
- **Attach reference**: upload a PNG, JPEG or WebP image (under 3 MB, at least 256 pixels each side), or paste one into the description box. The new image is made from it and your words.
- **Use current**: edit the post's current image. Describe the change, such as "make the background warmer", then click **Regenerate image**.
- **Change media**: **Find stock image**, **Use custom image URL**, or **Text only (keep image)**.
- **Download image**, and **Remove**, which deletes the image and its earlier versions for good.

Designing takes about a minute; **Stop generating** cancels it. You can move to other pages of the app meanwhile, but closing or reloading the tab stops it. Save text edits first, so the image matches the post.

A Reel or a Short has a video instead of an image: **Regenerate video**, **Try again** or **Generate video**, and **Download MP4**. A video keeps being made even if you close the tab. See [Short videos](/docs/publishing/posts/post-types#short-videos).

## See what the post cost

**AI cost for this post**, under the preview, shows the AI cost of everything done for this post: its **Cost**, **Tokens** and **Calls**, then each kind of work (**Writing**, **Revision**, **Image planning**, **Image**, **Video** and so on) with its tokens and cost. Rewrites and new images or videos are added as you make them. The run's research and ideas are shared by all its posts, so they are not included. Posts drafted before this was tracked say "No AI usage recorded for this post".

## Approve or reject

- **Approve**, or press <Kbd>A</Kbd>.
- **Reject**, or press <Kbd>R</Kbd>. Add a reason in **Why reject? Optional** if you like, then click **Reject post**. Your reason shapes future drafts.
- Press <Kbd>C</Kbd> to copy the post.

Shortcuts don't work while you're typing in a field. A rejected post can still be approved with **Approve instead**, and an approved post that hasn't gone out can be rejected with **Reject instead**. Rejecting cancels any scheduled publishing.

Once approved, the top bar offers **Publish now** and **Schedule**, or **Connect LinkedIn** (or the post's platform) if its account isn't connected. See [Schedule](/docs/publishing/schedule).

## When a post is locked

Once a post is being published, is published, or is marked as posted on Schedule, its text, image (or video) and post type can no longer change. **Regenerate** disappears, and earlier versions can be read but not restored. A note says why, for example "This post is published on LinkedIn, so its image can no longer change." Marked it as posted by mistake? Click **Not posted yet** on [Schedule](/schedule).

## Delete a post

Click the bin in the top bar and confirm **Delete post**. The post is removed from the app for good, with its image and alternates, and any scheduled publishing is cancelled. You can't delete a post while it is being published.

<Tags type="warning">

Deleting a post here doesn't remove it from the platform. If it was already published, delete it on the platform itself.

</Tags>
