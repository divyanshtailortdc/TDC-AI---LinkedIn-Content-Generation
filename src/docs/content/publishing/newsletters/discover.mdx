# Discover

Discover finds newsletters that fit your identity, so its inbox fills with current news from your field, which the engine can then use as evidence for ideas.

## How newsletters fit in

1. **Discover** finds newsletters on your topics and links to each one's signup page.
2. You sign up on the publisher's own page with your identity's inbox address.
3. New issues arrive in the [Inbox](/newsletters/inbox), where their stories are read and added to your Knowledge base.

<Tags type="important">

The app never subscribes for you. As the page says, you subscribe on each publisher's own page. The status on each card is only a record of what you tell the app.

</Tags>

## Find newsletters

Discovery searches from the identity's profile, so the profile needs something to go on. If the identity has no industry, offerings or audience yet, you see **Add your industry, offerings or audience to your identity first, so discovery knows what to look for.** Fill these in on the [Profile](/identity) first.

1. Click **Discover Newsletters**.
2. Wait while it runs. A card shows how long it has been going and what it does: **Search the web**, **Read publisher pages** to find their signup forms, and **Score each one** against your business. This usually takes about a minute, and can take up to five.
3. A message tells you how many it found and how many are new to your list. If nothing fitted well enough, you see **No new newsletters**. Try again later, or add more detail to your identity.

You can run discovery as often as you like. Newsletters already on your list get their details and score refreshed, but their status never changes, and newsletters you removed are never added back.

Discovery uses AI, so it counts towards your [AI usage](/docs/help/ai-usage). If you have reached a limit, discovery stops and tells you so.

## Your list at a glance

Four figures sum up your list: **Found** (and across how many categories), **New** (not subscribed yet, with how many you saved), **Subscribed** (and how many still await confirmation) and **Avg. relevance** (with how many are strong fits, 80 or more).

To narrow the list:

- **Status chips**: **All**, **New** (discovered and saved), **Subscribed** (including those awaiting confirmation) or **Unsubscribed / failed**, each with its count.
- **Search name, publisher or topic**: finds newsletters by any of these.
- **Category**: one category, or **All**.
- **Sort**: **Best fit** first, or **Newest**.

## What each card shows

| On the card        | What it tells you                                                                                       |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Category           | **Industry**, **Niche**, **Business publication** or **Technology**                                     |
| Status             | Where you stand with this newsletter (see below)                                                        |
| **Relevance**      | How well it fits your profile, out of 100: **Strong fit** (80 or more), **Good fit** or **Partial fit** |
| **Why it fits**    | A short reason for the score                                                                            |
| Topics             | What the newsletter covers                                                                              |
| Links              | The publisher's website and its signup page                                                             |
| **Found**          | When it was first found                                                                                 |
| **Feed available** | The newsletter also publishes a feed                                                                    |

## Subscribe to a newsletter

1. Copy your identity's inbox address: click **Copy** beside **Sign up with** and the address. If you haven't chosen one yet, click **Choose it in Inbox** first.
2. Click **Open signup page** on the card. The publisher's page opens in a new tab.
3. Sign up there with the inbox address, not your own email. Only mail sent to that address reaches the app.
4. Return to the card. It now asks **How did it go?**:
   - **I'm subscribed** if you are on the list.
   - **Waiting for confirmation email** if the publisher has to confirm first.
   - **Couldn't sign up** if the form failed, the list is closed or it needs payment.

Many publishers send a confirmation email. It arrives in the [Inbox](/newsletters/inbox) like any other email. While a newsletter awaits confirmation, its card says so and offers **Confirm in Inbox**. Open the email and click its confirmation link (links open in a new tab), then click **Mark as subscribed** on the card.

<Tags type="tip">

Use the inbox address for every signup. Issues sent to your personal email never reach the engine, so their stories can't be used for ideas.

</Tags>

## Newsletter statuses

| Status                    | Meaning                                                               |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Discovered**            | Found by discovery. You haven't done anything with it yet.            |
| **Saved**                 | You kept it for later with **Save**. Click **Saved** to un-save it.   |
| **Awaiting confirmation** | You signed up and are waiting for the publisher's confirmation email. |
| **Subscribed**            | You are on the list.                                                  |
| **Unsubscribed**          | You left the list.                                                    |
| **Signup failed**         | You couldn't sign up.                                                 |

Each card has one main button for its status: **Open signup page** until you are signed up, **Confirm in Inbox** while you await confirmation, and none once you are subscribed. The other buttons only show where they make sense for the status, for example **Save**, **Mark as subscribed** or **Mark as unsubscribed**.

<Tags type="note">

**Mark as unsubscribed** here does not change the Inbox. To stop a newsletter's stories being used for ideas, open one of its emails in the Inbox and click **I unsubscribed**. See [Inbox](/docs/publishing/newsletters/inbox).

</Tags>

## Remove a newsletter

Click the bin on a card, then **Remove**. The newsletter is taken off your list, and discovering again will not add it back.

<Details summary="The Discover Newsletters button is greyed out. Why?">

Discovery isn't set up on the server: it needs AI and web search. The page shows a notice naming what is missing. Ask your administrator to finish the setup.

</Details>
