# Published Content

Published Content lists everything the active identity has published, newest first: social media posts and blogs & articles together. Each item shows what it is, where it went out and when. It links to the item's page in the app and, when the address is known, to the live page.

<Eyebrow href="/published">Published Content</Eyebrow>

## Content types and destinations

The Content Engine makes two kinds of content. Each goes out to its own kind of destination:

| Content type         | Destinations                                               | How it goes out                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Social Media**     | LinkedIn, X, Threads, Facebook and Instagram               | Published through the accounts you connect, or posted by you and marked as posted  |
| **Blogs & Articles** | Your website, WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, or another site | Pasted into your site by you, then marked as published with where it went and when |

WordPress, Webflow and Shopify are places a blog is published, not kinds of content, and they are never offered for social media posts. The app does not publish to them for you: you paste the blog in, then record where it went.

## What appears here

- A **social media post** appears once it is published through your connected account, or once you mark it as posted. If you delete it from its platform through the app, it leaves this list.
- A **blog or article** appears once you mark it as published. Marking it as a draft again takes it off the list.

A post you hid from the Social Media Posts list still appears here: hiding only tidies that list.

## Filter the list

- **All**, **Social Media** and **Blogs & Articles** show one content type at a time, each with how many items it holds.
- The destination filters show only the platforms and sites something went out to, such as **LinkedIn** or **WordPress**, each with its count. Pick one to see only what went there. **All destinations** shows everything again.
- Blogs marked published before the app asked where they went are under **Not recorded**. To record one's destination, open the blog, click **More**, choose **Edit publishing details…**, pick where it is published and click **Save details**.

The filters are kept in the page address, so reloading or sharing the link keeps them.

## Each item

- **The mark**: the platform's logo for a post, or a globe for a website or CMS.
- **The title**: a post's opening line, or a blog's title. Click it, or **Open**, to open the post or the blog in the app.
- **What it is**: such as **LinkedIn post**, **X thread**, **Blog post** or **Article**, where a blog went, and the day it was published.
- **View live** opens the published page in a new tab when its address is known. For a post, that is the link the platform returned when it was published through your account. For a blog, it is the address you gave when you marked it published.
