# Welcome to the Content Engine

The **TDC Content Engine** plans and drafts social media posts for you or your business, and leaves every decision to you. It researches what is happening in your field, proposes post ideas that serve your goals, and drafts the posts you approve in your own voice. Nothing is published until you say so.

It works for LinkedIn, X, Threads, Facebook and Instagram, and for as many identities as you write for: yourself, your company, or a client.

## How it fits together

The engine follows the same path every time, and the app's sidebar is laid out in that order:

1. **Set up an identity.** Tell the engine who is posting, where, and how they sound: profile, voice, knowledge and preferences.
2. **Set your goals.** Say what your posts are for, such as finding clients or building a reputation, and on which platforms.
3. **Run.** A run researches your topics, including the newsletters your identity receives, and proposes ideas for your goals.
4. **Approve ideas.** You keep the ones worth writing about. Each comes with a scorecard explaining how strong it is and why.
5. **Create and edit posts.** You pick the approved ideas to write up, the platforms and the type of post. The engine drafts each in your voice, with an image or a carousel if you want one. You edit, check and approve.
6. **Schedule and publish.** Approved posts go out at the times you choose, through the accounts you connect.

## Finding your way around these docs

- Use the sections on the left, which follow the app's own sidebar.
- Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd> (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd> on a Mac) or <Kbd>/</Kbd> to search. Type a few words, or ask a question such as "how do I connect LinkedIn?". When you are signed in, questions are also matched by meaning, not only by the words they share with a page.
- In the app, the **?** button in the bottom-right corner has **Help for this page**, which opens the help page for the screen you are on.
