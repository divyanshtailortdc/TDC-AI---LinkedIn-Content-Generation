Each idea a run proposes waits for you to approve or reject it. Approving keeps it for a post; rejecting means later runs won't propose it again.

## How it fits together

It makes two kinds of content from the same identity, research and ideas: **social media posts** for LinkedIn, X, Threads, Facebook and Instagram, and **blogs & articles** for your own website. It works for as many identities as you write for: yourself, your company, or a client. The engine follows the same path every time, and the app's sidebar is laid out in that order:

1. **Set up an identity.** Tell the engine who is posting, where, and how they sound: profile, voice, knowledge and preferences.
2. **Set your goals.** Say what your content is for, such as finding clients or building a reputation, and on which platforms.
3. **Run.** A run researches your topics, including the newsletters your identity receives, and proposes ideas for your goals.
4. **Approve ideas.** You keep the ones worth writing about. Each comes with a scorecard explaining how strong it is and why.
5. **Create content.** For social media, you pick the approved ideas to write up, the platforms and the type of post. The engine drafts each in your voice, with an image or a carousel if you want one. For your website, it writes a blog or article from an idea or a topic: researched, cited and SEO-ready, in a document editor. You edit, check and approve.
6. **Publish.** Approved posts go out at the times you choose, through the accounts you connect. Blogs are pasted into your website, WordPress, Webflow, Shopify or another site, then marked as published. Published Content lists everything that is out.

A content type is what is made: a social media post, or a blog or article. A destination is where it goes out: a platform such as LinkedIn for a post, or a website or CMS such as WordPress for a blog.

<Tags type="important" label="You stay in control">

The engine suggests and drafts; you decide. Ideas wait for your approval, drafts wait for your review, and posts are only published through the accounts you connect yourself.
