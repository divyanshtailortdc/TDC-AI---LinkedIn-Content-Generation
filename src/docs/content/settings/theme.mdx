# Theme

The Theme page sets the colours of the app itself: its buttons, links and highlights, separately for light and dark mode.

<Tags type="note" label="Theme or Brand Theme?">

This page only changes how the app looks to you. The colours, logo and style of your post images are set on [Brand Theme](/docs/account-setup/brand-theme).

</Tags>

## Switch between light and dark mode

The sun and moon button at the top right of every page switches the app between light and dark mode. Its tooltip says **Switch to dark theme** or **Switch to light theme**. The same button is on the sign-in page and in these help pages.

Until you click it, the app follows your device's own light or dark setting. Once you choose, the app remembers your choice in this browser.

## Change the app's colours

The Theme page has a card for each mode, **Light mode** and **Dark mode**, and each card has two colours:

| Colour        | Where it is used                             |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary**   | Main buttons, links and focus rings.         |
| **Secondary** | The active menu item, highlights and badges. |

The colours are shown read-only until you start editing:

1. Click **Edit** at the top of the page.
2. For each colour, click the swatch to open your browser's colour picker, or type a colour code such as `#159bb2` into the box next to it.
3. Watch the **Preview** in each card. It shows an active and an inactive menu item, a primary button, a badge and a link in the colours you picked.
4. Click **Save changes**. You see **Theme saved**, and the whole app switches to the new colours.

Click **Cancel** to put back the colours you had before you started editing.

A colour code must have six digits after the `#`. While you type, the colour only changes once the code is complete; if you leave the box with an incomplete code, it goes back to the current colour.

### Readability warning

If a card says "Text on primary buttons may be hard to read in this colour.", the primary colour is too close to the page background. Text on main buttons is drawn in the background colour, so pick a darker primary colour for light mode or a lighter one for dark mode.

## Reset to the default colours

While editing, you can go back to the app's own colours:

- **Reset to default** in a card resets that mode only.
- **Reset all** at the top resets both modes.

Then click **Save changes**. Each reset button is greyed out when its colours already match the defaults.

The default colours are:

| Mode       | Primary                | Secondary              |
| ---------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Light mode | `#09090B` (near black) | `#159BB2` (teal)       |
| Dark mode  | `#FAFAFA` (near white) | `#22B5CD` (light teal) |

## Where your colours are kept

As the page says, your colours "are kept in this browser". They are not part of your account, so:

- Another browser or device shows the default colours until you set them there too.
- Clearing your browser's cookies for the app brings back the defaults.
- Other people who use the app are not affected by your colours.

There are no ready-made colour presets: you choose each colour yourself, or reset to the defaults.
